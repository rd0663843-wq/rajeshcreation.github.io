About Me

A bit about how I work, and what I believe in.

I'm Rajesh Das, a digital marketer and freelancer based in Barasat, West Bengal. My work sits at the intersection of strategy and execution — I plan campaigns, build systems, and stay close enough to the numbers to know what's actually working.

Alongside client work, I spend time mentoring people who are starting out in digital marketing, and building teams that can execute consistently rather than in bursts. I believe good marketing is less about tricks and more about showing up with a clear plan, day after day.

Off the desk, I'm usually reading — books on mindset, deep work, and building better habits shape how I approach both my business and the people I work with.
